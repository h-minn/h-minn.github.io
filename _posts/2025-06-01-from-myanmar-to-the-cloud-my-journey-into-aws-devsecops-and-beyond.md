# Hello, I'm Min!

I'm an **AWS Solutions Architect** and **DevSecOps enthusiast** currently based in Japan. Originally from Myanmar, I’ve been living and working here since 2019.

In this post, I’m excited to share my professional journey—from humble beginnings in software engineering to building scalable, secure cloud infrastructures, and what inspires me to keep pushing forward.

---

## 🚀 How It All Started

My journey into tech began with a love for problem-solving and a deep curiosity for how systems work.

After moving to Japan in 2019, I started my first job as a **Java System Engineer** at a local SIer. I gained hands-on experience in:

- QA engineering  
- Integration testing  
- Development workflows  
- Cloud migration projects

These early experiences sparked my fascination with **cloud computing**—specifically how it could dramatically improve speed and efficiency in system delivery. That’s when I decided to pivot toward **cloud infrastructure**.

I explored both **Azure** and **AWS**, eventually choosing AWS as my focus. Today, I work at an AWS-specialized cloud integrator where I dive deep into:

- Latest AWS services  
- DevOps tooling (CI/CD, GitOps)  
- Modern programming stacks

Over time, I recognized the growing importance of **security in DevOps**, which led me to fully embrace **DevSecOps** as a mindset and practice.

---

## 💪 Overcoming Challenges

Shifting careers in a new country and navigating a highly technical space came with its fair share of challenges:

- 🌐 Adapting to a new language and work culture  
- ☁️ Learning cloud technologies without a formal IT degree  
- ⚙️ Keeping pace with rapid changes in DevOps and cybersecurity

Here’s how I pushed through:

- 🧠 Daily study habits: documentation, blogs, hands-on labs  
- 🌍 Connecting with the global tech community  
- 🎓 Pursuing certifications to structure and validate my skills

---

## 🔐 Why I Love Cloud & DevSecOps

Cloud technologies continue to transform the way businesses operate. For me, designing **secure**, **scalable**, and **automated** solutions is incredibly rewarding.

I believe **DevSecOps** is more than just a buzzword — it's essential to building trust in modern software. Integrating security from day one helps protect users and businesses alike, and that’s a mission I’m proud to support.
