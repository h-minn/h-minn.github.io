## 🧩 My Personal Time Management Formula

Here’s what works best for me — after years of trial and error:

### ✅ 1. Time Blocking Across Calendars

Each week, I plan both my **work** and **personal** calendar using clear blocks:

- **Deep Work**: For focused coding, learning, or writing
- **Shallow Work**: Emails, admin, lightweight reviews
- **Family Time**: Sacred space in the evenings and weekends
- **Learning Zone**: 1–4 hours daily (depending on energy)

### ✅ 2. One Big Thing Rule

Every morning, I ask myself:

> _“What’s the ONE most important task I need to accomplish today?”_

That task goes to the top of my list. Everything else is secondary.

### ✅ 3. Digital Tools That Keep Me Sane

- 🕒 **Pomofocus** – 25-minute deep focus sessions  
- 🗓️ **Google Calendar** – shared with my family to plan life and study  
- 📝 **Google Keep** – quick brain-dump and idea capturing  

---

## 👶 Parenting + Tech Life = Embracing Flexibility

As a parent, my schedule needs to bend — not break.

Some real-life tactics that help me:
- Study during nap time or after bedtime  
- Automate tasks with scripts and cron jobs  
- Batch meetings to reduce context switching  
- Use home automation (cleaning robots, smart lights)  
- Shop smart: I grocery shop during off-peak weekday hours  

---

## 📚 How I Learn Without Burning Out

I’m passionate about learning, but I’ve learned not to overdo it.

Here’s my approach:
- **Micro-learning**: 30-minute sessions even on busy days  
- **Passive learning**: Tech podcasts during walks or drives  
- **Weekend deep dives**: 2–3 focused hours for bigger topics
